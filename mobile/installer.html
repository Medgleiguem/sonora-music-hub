About this app

Sonora is a music player for the songs you love. Search YouTube for songs, artists and playlists, or paste a YouTube or Spotify link. Like songs and build playlists without downloading anything, then download what you want to hear offline.

Read more

Every pasted playlist gives you two clear choices: add it to your library to play online, or download it to your phone. Spotify playlists, albums and songs are matched on YouTube one by one, and songs that are hard to find get a Search button so you can pick the right version.

Music keeps playing with the screen off and from Android's media controls. Shuffle, repeat, a queue, recently played, and curated Arabic classics are built in. Sonora opens full screen, and everything you save stays private on your phone.

Highlights

Online or offline

Stream right away, or download songs and whole playlists for when you have no connection.

Bring your playlists

Paste a Spotify or YouTube playlist, album, Mix or song link, and Sonora does the rest.

Plays in the background

Lock your phone or switch apps. Control playback from the notification and lock screen.

Likes without downloads

Heart any song, even from search. Download your Liked songs later in one tap.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your library never leaves your phone.

How to install

Download

Tap Install on your Android phone. The installer downloads to your phone.

Open it

Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser.

Install

Tap Install, or Update to keep your library. Then open Sonora and start listening.

Sonora is installed from this page, not from an app store. Music comes from YouTube and depends on YouTube’s connection: some songs can take a moment to start or may be unavailable. Only download content you have the right to use.
