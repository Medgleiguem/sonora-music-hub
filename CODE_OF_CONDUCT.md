# Code of Conduct

Sonora welcomes people of every background and experience level. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and clear. Assume contributors are learning parts of the project.
- Critique code and ideas with evidence; do not attack people.
- Respect boundaries, privacy and differences of opinion.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes openly.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized behavior, personal attacks, spam and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive GitHub content through GitHub's **Report content** control. The maintainer is [@Medgleiguem](https://github.com/Medgleiguem). Do not publish private reports or personal information in an issue. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, close or lock threads, warn participants, or restrict participation according to the severity and repetition of the behavior. Reports should be handled fairly and shared only as needed for investigation. No response-time guarantee is made.
